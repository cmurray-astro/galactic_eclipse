Age bin

Fundamental-mode Cepheids from the OGLE-IV catalog with VMC near-infrared photometry; ultra-long-period Cepheids (P > 80 d) are excluded, as are likely blends, identified by a poor Gaia DR3 astrometric fit (RUWE > 1.4; Lindegren et al. 2021; Belokurov et al. 2020). Distances use the Ripepi et al. (2017) SMC fundamental-mode WJK slope (a = −3.334, fitted above the PL break at P = 2.95 d and applied to all periods), with the zero-point set so that the sample's mean distance modulus corresponds to d₀ = 62.0 kpc. Ages are from the Bono et al. (2005) period–age relation for SMC metallicity. All Cepheids in the analysis sample are plotted. RA is plotted with east to the left, matching sky convention. Rendered on a plain 2D canvas (no WebGL) for maximum compatibility.
