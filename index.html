Age bin (Table 1)

Fundamental-mode Cepheids from the OGLE-IV catalog with VMC near-infrared photometry; ultra-long-period Cepheids (P > 80 d) are excluded. Distances use the Ripepi et al. (2017) SMC fundamental-mode WJK slope (a = −3.334, fitted above the PL break at P = 2.95 d and applied to all periods), with the zero-point set so that the sample's mean distance modulus corresponds to d₀ = 62.0 kpc. Ages are from the Bono et al. (2005) period–age relation for SMC metallicity. Only Cepheids at 44–82 kpc are plotted, which omits ~2% of the sample from view (not from the analysis). RA is plotted with east to the left, matching sky convention. Rendered on a plain 2D canvas (no WebGL) for maximum compatibility.
