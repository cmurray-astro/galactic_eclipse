Age bin

Fundamental-mode Cepheids from the OGLE-IV catalog with VMC near-infrared photometry; ultra-long-period Cepheids (P > 80 d) are excluded, as are likely blends, identified by a poor Gaia DR3 astrometric fit (RUWE > 1.4; Lindegren et al. 2021; Belokurov et al. 2020). Distances use the Ripepi et al. (2017) SMC fundamental-mode WJK slope (a = −3.334, fitted above the PL break at P = 2.95 d and applied to all periods), with the zero-point set so that the sample's mean distance modulus corresponds to d₀ = 62.0 kpc. Ages are from the Bono et al. (2005) period–age relation for SMC metallicity. Only Cepheids at 44–82 kpc are plotted, which omits ~1% of the sample from view (not from the analysis); the counts in the legend are for the full sample. RA is plotted with east to the left, matching sky convention. Rendered on a plain 2D canvas (no WebGL) for maximum compatibility.
